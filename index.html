Freedom, pending approval

Today the country marks the day it stopped waiting for permission. Here are the things I'm still waiting on.

Article I

The Saturday that isn't mine

A six-day week doesn't take one day. It takes the evening before it and the morning after it too.

There's no stretch long enough to travel, to see family properly, to build something of my own, or to simply stop.

A five-day week isn't time off from the work. It's what makes me good at it on Monday.

Article II

Tickets raised, tickets unread

The HRMS exists so requests have a paper trail. Mine have the trail and nothing at the end of it — raised, logged, then quiet.

No owner, no status change, not even a rejection I could argue with.

A system that accepts requests but never answers them isn't a process. It's a waiting room with no door.

Article III

Sent, delivered, unanswered

Emails go out and weeks go past. I follow up. I follow up again. Meanwhile the thing that needed a decision sits still, and so do I.

I'd rather have a "no" in two days than silence for three weeks. A reply costs a minute. Not replying costs somebody else's month.
